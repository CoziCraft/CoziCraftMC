# Welcome To CoziCraft

The CoziCraft community portal is taking shape.

This first version focuses on the foundation: a clear server address, public joining information, editable wiki content, FAQs, rules, news, and a store page that can later link to Tebex.

The goal is simple: make it easy for players to find the right information and easy for maintainers to keep that information fresh.
