# Ranks

Use this page to document player ranks, perks, and progression after they are confirmed.

## Editing Guidance

- Keep rank benefits accurate and easy to compare.
- Avoid promising perks that are not live.
- Mention whether a rank is earned, staff-granted, or store-related.
- Review store-related rank language against Minecraft's usage and monetization guidelines.
