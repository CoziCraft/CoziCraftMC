# Claims

Claims protect player builds and help everyone understand where they can safely build.

## Player Expectations

- Claim important builds early.
- Do not build inside or against someone else's claimed area without permission.
- Leave breathing room around towns, farms, and active projects.
- Ask staff if abandoned builds need review.
