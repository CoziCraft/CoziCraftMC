# Rules Wiki

This wiki page gives maintainers a place to expand the public rules without making the main rules page hard to scan.

## Editing Guidance

- Keep rules direct and specific.
- Put examples under the rule they clarify.
- Avoid private moderation processes or staff-only notes.
- Use neutral language that is easy to enforce consistently.

## Common Rule Areas

- Chat behavior
- Build respect
- Land claims
- Trading and shops
- Client mods and automation
- Event conduct
